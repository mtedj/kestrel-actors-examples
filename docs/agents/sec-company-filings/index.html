SEC Filings - EDGAR Forms, 10-K, 10-Q, Dates & 公司公告

Collect SEC EDGAR filings by company CIK: 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K and other forms. Each filing includes company, accession number, form, filing/report dates and original document link. Free date/form filters and ID/hash exclusions. Bounded archive pages; no filing-text extraction. 公司公告、财报。

{
  "actor": "kestrel/sec-company-filings",
  "input": {
    "ciks": [
      "320193"
    ],
    "forms": [],
    "sinceDate": "",
    "untilDate": "",
    "maxResults": 3,
    "maxPages": 1
  },

    "maxTotalChargeUsd": 0.25,

  "waitSecs": 30
